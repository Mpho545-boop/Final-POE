Who we are

A caring place for pets in need.

Our mission is to give vulnerable dogs and cats a safe place to recover while helping them find responsible, loving homes.

Our mission

Paws & Hearts Animal Shelter is a fictional community shelter created for this website project. It represents an organisation that supports abandoned, lost and neglected pets.

We believe animals should be treated with patience, respect and kindness. Our goal is to provide temporary care and help each pet move towards a safe permanent home.

Our values

  • Compassion and respect for every animal.
  • Responsible adoption and lifelong commitment.
  • Honesty, teamwork and community involvement.
  • Safe, clean and healthy shelter spaces.
